Player character bisa membuka ability menggunakan [[SatisfactionPoint|satisfaction point]] yang dimilikinya
Player character bisa membuka ability hanya jika [[SatisfactionPoint|satisfaction point]]-nya mencukupi [[UnlockRequirement|unlock requirement]] dari suatu ability
