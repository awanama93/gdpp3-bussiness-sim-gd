## Day performance

Ketika evaluasi day dilakukan, makan akan dinilai juga day performance bonus
Berikut adalah pengukuran day performance bonus

| Performance | At least mid serve | Satisfaction point bonus |
| ----------- | ------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Excellent   | 7 - 10             | +5                       |
| Fine        | 4 - 6              | 0                        |
| Meh         | 2 - 3              | -1                       |
