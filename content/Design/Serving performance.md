## Serving performance

Ketika evaluasi service dilakukan, performa player juga akan dinilai
Berikut adalah pengukuran performa player

| Performance | Satisfaction point | Satisfaction point bonus |
| ----------- | ------------------ | ------------------------ |
| Good        | 7 - 10             | +5                       |
| Mid         | 4 - 6              | 0                        |
| Bad         | 2 - 3              | -1                       |
